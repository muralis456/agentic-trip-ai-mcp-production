# General Trip Safety Basics

Use official government and destination sources for current safety advisories, entry rules, visa requirements and health requirements.

For general planning, keep emergency contact information available, protect passports and payment cards, use reputable transportation, and share important itinerary details with a trusted person when appropriate.

When a user asks for current travel advisories or legal/entry requirements, AgenticTripAI should treat the knowledge base as a starting point and retrieve current authoritative information rather than presenting this document as the latest official policy.

# Australia — Sydney and Melbourne Destination Knowledge

Destination: Australia
Category: destination

## Trip planning
Sydney is suited to harbor experiences, beaches, coastal walks and city attractions. Melbourne is known for food, arts, sports, laneways and nearby regional excursions.

## Planning guidance
- Account for the large travel distance from India and plan recovery time after arrival.
- Cluster city attractions geographically.
- For outdoor activities, check local weather and conditions before departure.
- Domestic flights may be useful for longer Australia itineraries.

## Safety
Follow local beach, hiking, wildlife and fire-safety guidance. Conditions can change quickly in outdoor environments.

Current visa rules, domestic schedules, weather, fire warnings, road conditions and attraction information require live sources.

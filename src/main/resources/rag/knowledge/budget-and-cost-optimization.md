# Travel Budget and Cost Optimization

## Budget categories
1. International/domestic transport.
2. Accommodation.
3. Local transportation.
4. Food and drinks.
5. Attractions and activities.
6. Visa/entry costs.
7. Insurance.
8. Taxes/fees.
9. Shopping/miscellaneous.
10. Emergency contingency.

## Optimization strategies
- Compare total trip cost, not individual prices.
- Shift dates when the user is flexible.
- Compare nearby airports.
- Use public transport where practical.
- Choose accommodation near high-value activities.
- Bundle only when the bundle genuinely lowers cost or improves flexibility.
- Protect a contingency reserve rather than spending the entire budget.

## Currency
Display the currency used by the user and clearly identify exchange-rate assumptions. Live exchange rates should come from a current currency service rather than static RAG data.

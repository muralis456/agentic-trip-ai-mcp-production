# Rail and Road Trip Planning

## Rail planning
Compare train duration, frequency, station location, seat class, reservations, luggage rules and cancellation conditions.

## Road trips
Consider driving time, road quality, tolls, fuel, parking, weather, border crossings and driver fatigue.

## Route design
Avoid excessive daily driving. Add scenic stops only when they do not compromise required arrival times.

## Live data
Current timetables, road closures, border conditions and fares must be checked with live sources.

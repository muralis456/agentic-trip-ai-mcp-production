# Japan — Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka Destination Knowledge

Destination: Japan
Category: destination

## Trip planning
Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka can be combined for a first-time Japan itinerary. Tokyo is strong for neighborhoods, technology, food and museums; Kyoto for temples, gardens and traditional culture; Osaka for food and urban entertainment.

## Planning guidance
- Avoid changing hotels every night; use rail connections and strategic bases.
- Kyoto attractions can become crowded, so start important sightseeing early.
- Build weather and walking breaks into daily plans.
- Rail travel is efficient, but route and fare choices should be checked for the exact itinerary.

## Culture
Quiet behavior on public transport, queueing, careful waste handling and respectful conduct at temples and shrines are important.

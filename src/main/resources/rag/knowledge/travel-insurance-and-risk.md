# Travel Insurance and Risk Planning

Insurance coverage varies by policy and country. Travelers should read the actual policy wording.

## Common coverage categories
- Trip cancellation/interruption.
- Medical expenses.
- Emergency evacuation.
- Baggage loss/delay.
- Travel delay.
- Personal liability.

## Planning principle
Recommend considering insurance based on trip cost, destination, activities, health/age factors and cancellation exposure, without claiming that a specific policy covers a situation unless verified.

## Agentic use
Risk can be represented as metadata and used by the planner to increase buffers, prefer flexible fares/hotels and add human approval for high-risk recommendations.

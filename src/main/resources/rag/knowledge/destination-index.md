This knowledge base contains durable destination-planning guidance for selected destinations. It is not a source of live facts.

- Singapore
- Thailand: Bangkok and Phuket
- Japan: Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka
- Europe: Paris, Switzerland and Italy
- USA: New York and California

- Sri Lanka
- Nepal: Kathmandu and Pokhara
- Malaysia: Kuala Lumpur and Langkawi
- Vietnam: Hanoi, Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City

## Retrieval guidance
When a destination is present in the user request, prefer destination-specific documents before generic travel guidance. Combine them with general knowledge documents for packing, budgeting, transport, culture and safety.

## Live-data boundary
Do not treat these documents as authoritative for current visa rules, travel advisories, weather, flight/hotel availability, prices, transport schedules, attraction hours, strikes, closures or safety alerts. Those should be verified using current authoritative sources and/or MCP tools.

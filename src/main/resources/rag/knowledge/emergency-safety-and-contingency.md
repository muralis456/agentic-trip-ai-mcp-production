# Travel Safety and Contingency Planning

This is general planning guidance. For emergencies, use local emergency services and official government guidance.

## Before departure
- Save passport/visa copies securely.
- Record travel insurance and emergency contacts.
- Confirm accommodation and transport details.
- Check official travel advisories for the destination.
- Share an itinerary with a trusted contact when appropriate.

## During travel
- Maintain awareness of local laws and customs.
- Keep backups of essential documents.
- Avoid depending on a single payment method or communication channel.
- Keep contingency time around important departures.

## Disruption handling
For cancellations, severe weather or strikes, the agent should prioritize safety and then re-optimize transport, accommodation and itinerary constraints using live data.

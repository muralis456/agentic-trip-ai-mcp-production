# Nepal — Kathmandu and Pokhara Destination Knowledge

Destination: Nepal
Category: destination

## Trip planning
Kathmandu is useful for cultural and heritage experiences. Pokhara is a base for mountain views, lakes and trekking-related trips.

## Planning guidance
- Keep weather buffers for mountain and trekking plans.
- Avoid assuming road travel times are short in mountainous areas.
- Trekking itineraries should include acclimatization and contingency days where appropriate.
- Use local guides or authorized operators for activities that require specialist knowledge.

## Safety
Altitude, weather, terrain and transport conditions can materially affect plans.

## RAG boundary
Current trekking permits, weather, road conditions, flight schedules and entry rules must be verified with live sources.

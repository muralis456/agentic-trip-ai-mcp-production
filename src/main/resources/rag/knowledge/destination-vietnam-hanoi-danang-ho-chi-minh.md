# Vietnam — Hanoi, Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City Knowledge

Destination: Vietnam
Category: destination

## Trip planning
Vietnam offers strong food, culture, history, beaches and city experiences. Hanoi, Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City create different trip styles and can be connected by domestic transport.

## Planning guidance
- Select destinations based on trip duration instead of trying to cover the whole country.
- Street food and local markets are major experiences, but travelers should use sensible food and water hygiene.
- Da Nang can be paired with nearby cultural sites and beach time.
- Keep buffers for domestic transfers.

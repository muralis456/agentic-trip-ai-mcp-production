# USA — New York and California Destination Knowledge

Destination: USA
Category: destination

## Trip planning
New York is suited to museums, neighborhoods, food, Broadway and urban sightseeing. California supports varied trips including San Francisco, Los Angeles, coastal drives and national parks.

## Planning guidance
- Treat the USA as a large destination; domestic travel time can be substantial.
- For California road trips, avoid overloading the itinerary with long daily drives.
- In cities, group activities by neighborhood.
- For national parks, check permits, road conditions and seasonal access before finalizing plans.

## Transport
Urban transit is useful in major cities, while rental cars can be practical for many regional itineraries.

## RAG boundary
Current entry rules, park access, road closures, weather, hotel prices and transport schedules must be verified live.

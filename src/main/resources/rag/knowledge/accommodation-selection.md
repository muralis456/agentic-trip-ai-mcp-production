# Accommodation Selection Knowledge

## Compare hotels on
- Location relative to planned activities.
- Room type and occupancy.
- Total price including taxes/fees where known.
- Breakfast inclusion.
- Cancellation policy.
- Check-in/check-out time.
- Reviews and recent review trends.
- Public transport access.
- Safety and neighborhood suitability.
- Accessibility requirements.

## Hidden cost checks
Look for resort/destination fees, city taxes, parking, breakfast, extra-bed charges, cleaning fees and deposits where applicable.

## Location trade-off
A cheaper hotel far from attractions may cost more in local transport and time. Optimize total trip cost and convenience, not room price alone.

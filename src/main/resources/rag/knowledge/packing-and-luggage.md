# Packing and Luggage Planning

Packing should be based on destination, weather, duration, activities, baggage rules and traveler needs.

## Categories
- Documents and payment methods.
- Clothing layers.
- Weather protection.
- Toiletries.
- Electronics and chargers.
- Medicines and prescriptions where applicable.
- Activity-specific equipment.
- Small emergency items.

## Flight baggage
Never assume baggage allowance. Retrieve the actual fare/airline allowance from live flight information or the airline's current terms.

## Packing strategy
Use a capsule approach for longer trips, plan laundry access and keep essential items in carry-on when appropriate.

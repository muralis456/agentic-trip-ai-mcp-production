# Visa, Passport and Entry Requirements

Entry rules are country- and nationality-specific and can change quickly. This document provides a planning checklist, not legal or immigration advice. Always verify current requirements with the destination immigration authority, embassy/consulate or official government source before travel.

## Checklist
- Passport validity requirement.
- Blank passport pages if required.
- Visa or visa-waiver eligibility.
- Electronic travel authorization or pre-registration.
- Transit visa requirements for connecting airports.
- Proof of onward/return travel where required.
- Accommodation details.
- Financial proof where required.
- Travel insurance where required.
- Vaccination or health documentation where officially required.
- Minor/child consent and documentation where applicable.

## RAG usage
Visa content is high-risk for stale information. Store source URL, issuing authority, publication/update date and destination country in metadata. The agent should retrieve the latest source and clearly distinguish general guidance from verified current rules.

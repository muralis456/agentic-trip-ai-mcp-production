# Weather and Seasonality Planning

Weather data is time-sensitive and should come from live weather tools for near-term trips. RAG is useful for general seasonal planning.

## Planning dimensions
- Temperature range.
- Rainfall probability and seasonal rainfall patterns.
- Snow/ice conditions.
- Humidity.
- Heat-wave or storm season.
- Daylight hours.
- Peak versus shoulder versus low season.

## Practical rules
- Use forecast data for the actual travel dates when available.
- Keep outdoor activities flexible in volatile weather.
- Do not infer exact weather from historical averages.
- Build indoor alternatives for destinations with high weather variability.
- For mountains/coasts, account for microclimates and rapid changes.

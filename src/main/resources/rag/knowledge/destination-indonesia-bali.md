# Bali, Indonesia — Destination Knowledge

Destination: Bali, Indonesia
Category: destination

## Trip planning
Bali supports beach holidays, wellness, temples, food, rice terraces, waterfalls and cultural experiences. Different areas offer different trip styles, so choosing a base matters.

## Planning guidance
- Ubud is useful for culture, nature and inland experiences; southern areas are more beach and nightlife oriented.
- Road travel can be slow, so avoid scheduling too many distant attractions in one day.
- Allow flexible time for rain when planning outdoor activities.
- Respect temple dress and behavior requirements.

## RAG boundary
Current entry rules, ferry/flight schedules, weather, marine conditions, local prices and attraction rules must be verified live.

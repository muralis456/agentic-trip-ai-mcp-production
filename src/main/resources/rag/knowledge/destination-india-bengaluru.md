# Bengaluru, India — Destination Knowledge

Destination: Bengaluru (Bangalore), Karnataka, India
Category: destination

## Trip planning
Bengaluru works well as a city break and as a gateway for Karnataka road trips. Common trip themes include technology and modern city life, parks, food, cafes, heritage, and nearby hill or wildlife destinations.

## Planning guidance
- Group attractions by neighborhood to reduce cross-city travel.
- Keep traffic buffers for airport and intercity transfers.
- Combine indoor activities with outdoor plans because weather can change quickly.
- For weekend trips, consider nearby destinations such as Mysuru, Coorg, Chikmagalur, Hampi, or Kabini based on available time.

## Food and culture
Bengaluru has strong South Indian food traditions alongside regional Indian and international cuisines. Respect local customs at temples and heritage sites and follow venue-specific photography rules.

## Transport
Metro, taxis, app-based cabs, buses and private vehicles are common options. Airport transfers should include generous traffic buffers.

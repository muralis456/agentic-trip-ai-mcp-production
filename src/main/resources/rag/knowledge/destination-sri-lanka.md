# Sri Lanka — Destination Knowledge

Destination: Sri Lanka
Category: destination

## Trip planning
Sri Lanka works well for compact multi-stop itineraries combining beaches, cultural sites, wildlife and hill-country scenery.

## Planning guidance
- Avoid excessive one-day road transfers.
- Combine the cultural triangle with hill country or coast depending on trip length.
- Wildlife safaris should follow park rules and responsible wildlife practices.
- Leave contingency time for road and rail variability.

## Culture
Respect religious sites, local dress expectations and wildlife protection rules.

## RAG boundary
Current entry rules, weather, park conditions, transport schedules and accommodation prices must be checked live.

# Travel Packing and Local Experience Guidance

## Packing

Pack according to destination climate, trip length and planned activities. Comfortable walking shoes are useful for city itineraries. Carry required medication, identification, payment methods and copies of important travel documents. Keep a small day bag for water, chargers and weather protection.

## Local experiences

Local markets, neighborhood food, cultural districts, walking tours and public-transit experiences can make an itinerary more representative of the destination. Avoid overloading every day with attractions; leave time for spontaneous exploration.

## Respecting local culture

Travelers should check local etiquette, dress expectations, photography restrictions and rules for religious or cultural sites before visiting. When the application has an official destination document, that document should take precedence over this general guidance.

# Malaysia — Kuala Lumpur and Langkawi Destination Knowledge

Destination: Malaysia
Category: destination

## Trip planning
Kuala Lumpur supports city sightseeing, food and shopping. Langkawi is suited to beaches, nature and relaxed resort travel.

## Planning guidance
- Combine central Kuala Lumpur attractions geographically.
- Use Langkawi for a slower-paced segment after a city itinerary.
- Keep flexible indoor alternatives for tropical rain.
- Compare flight and transfer combinations for island travel.

## Culture
Malaysia is multicultural. Respect religious sites, local customs and venue-specific dress requirements.

## RAG boundary
Current entry rules, ferry/flight schedules, weather, attraction hours and prices require live verification.

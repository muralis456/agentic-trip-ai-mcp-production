# Europe — Paris, Switzerland and Italy Destination Knowledge

Destination: Europe multi-country
Category: destination

## Trip planning
Multi-country European trips benefit from geographic clustering and limiting the number of hotel changes. Paris works well for museums, architecture and food; Switzerland for alpine scenery and rail journeys; Italy for cities, history, food and regional experiences.

## Planning guidance
- Compare rail and air options for each leg rather than assuming one mode is always cheaper.
- Protect time for border or station transfers and luggage handling.
- Use regional bases where day trips are practical.
- Check reservation requirements for popular museums, trains and attractions.

## Budget
Accommodation and intercity transport can dominate costs. Separate the budget into fixed transport, accommodation, food, attractions and contingency.

## RAG boundary
Schengen/entry rules, train schedules, strikes, attraction hours, weather and prices are time-sensitive and must be verified live.

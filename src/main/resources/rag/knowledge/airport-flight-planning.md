# Airport and Flight Planning Knowledge

## Flight search factors
Compare more than ticket price:
- Total journey time.
- Number and duration of connections.
- Airport changes during a connection.
- Baggage allowance.
- Fare conditions and change/cancellation rules.
- Departure and arrival times.
- Self-transfer versus protected connection.
- Airport transfer time and cost.
- Overnight connections.

## Connection planning
For separate tickets, allow substantially more buffer because the traveler may need to collect baggage, clear immigration/customs and check in again. Never assume a minimum connection time for a specific airport without checking the airport/airline rules.

## Airport transfer
Consider airport-to-city distance, traffic, rail/metro availability, operating hours and late-night arrival. For international arrivals, include immigration and baggage time in the itinerary.

## Agent behavior
Use live flight/MCP data for current schedules and prices. Use RAG for stable planning knowledge such as connection-risk concepts and airport-transfer planning principles.

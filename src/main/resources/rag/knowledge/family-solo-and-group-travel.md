# Family, Solo and Group Travel Planning

## Family travel
Consider child age, stroller access, room occupancy, meal timing, attraction suitability, nap/rest periods and transport convenience.

## Solo travel
Consider neighborhood safety, late-night arrival, public transport, accommodation reputation, emergency contacts and communication options.

## Group travel
Capture the group size, room configuration, shared versus individual budgets, transportation capacity and decision-making constraints. Keep some flexibility for different interests.

## Agent behavior
The planner should treat traveler type as a constraint that can change hotel, activity and transport ranking.

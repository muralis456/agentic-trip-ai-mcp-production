# Cultural Etiquette and Responsible Travel

## General principles
- Respect local laws, religious practices and dress expectations.
- Ask before photographing people when appropriate.
- Follow rules at religious, heritage and protected sites.
- Avoid treating local communities as attractions.
- Support legitimate local businesses.
- Minimize waste and respect environmental restrictions.

## Responsible recommendations
Prefer experiences that are legal, ethical and respectful of wildlife and local communities. Do not recommend prohibited activities simply because they are popular online.

## RAG usage
Culture knowledge benefits from source attribution and destination-specific retrieval. Generic etiquette should not override current local rules.

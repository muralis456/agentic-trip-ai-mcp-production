# Singapore — Destination Knowledge

Destination: Singapore
Category: destination

## Trip planning
Singapore is a compact destination suited to family trips, food-focused travel, gardens, museums, neighborhoods and efficient urban sightseeing.

## Planning guidance
- Combine nearby areas such as Marina Bay, Civic District and Chinatown rather than crossing the island repeatedly.
- Use indoor attractions during heavy rain or very humid periods.
- Reserve timed-entry attractions where required.
- Hawker centres provide a useful way to experience local food at different price points.

## Transport
MRT and buses are the backbone of local travel; walking can be efficient between nearby districts.

## Culture and etiquette
Singapore places strong emphasis on public cleanliness and orderly behavior. Follow local rules, venue instructions and restrictions on activities that may be prohibited.

## RAG boundary
Current entry rules, attraction hours, public transport disruptions, weather and prices require live verification.

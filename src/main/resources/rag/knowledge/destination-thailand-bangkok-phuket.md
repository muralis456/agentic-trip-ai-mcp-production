# Thailand — Bangkok and Phuket Destination Knowledge

Destination: Thailand
Category: destination

## Trip planning
Bangkok is strong for temples, food, markets, shopping and urban experiences. Phuket is suited to beaches, island excursions and resort-oriented trips.

## Planning guidance
- Avoid packing too many Bangkok attractions into one day; heat and traffic can make transfers slow.
- For Phuket, reserve a weather-flexible day for boat or island activities.
- Temple visits require respectful clothing and behavior.
- Keep buffers around airport and ferry transfers.

## Transport
Bangkok has rail systems, taxis and other local transport. Phuket trips often use taxis, transfers or tours. Verify current fares and schedules.

## RAG boundary
Current visa rules, ferry schedules, weather, marine conditions, attraction hours and prices must be checked live.

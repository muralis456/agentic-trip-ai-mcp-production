# Local Transport and Mobility

## Options
- Metro/subway.
- Local rail.
- Bus/tram.
- Taxi and ride-hailing.
- Airport express services.
- Rental car.
- Intercity rail/bus.
- Walking and cycling.

## Selection criteria
Evaluate cost, travel time, operating hours, luggage convenience, reliability, accessibility and safety.

## Rental car considerations
Check driving side, license requirements, insurance, tolls, parking availability, fuel policy and local traffic restrictions. Avoid recommending a rental car in dense cities unless it provides a clear benefit.

## Itinerary rule
Estimate door-to-door time rather than only the vehicle's scheduled travel time.

# Food, Dining and Local Experiences

## Recommendation dimensions
- Local specialties.
- Dietary requirements.
- Budget level.
- Neighborhood and opening hours.
- Reservation requirements.
- Tourist versus local-oriented experiences.
- Family suitability.
- Alcohol-free or other preference constraints.

## Food safety
Use official/local public-health guidance where relevant. Avoid claiming a restaurant is safe for a specific allergy unless the establishment can confirm its processes.

## Experience planning
Mix major attractions with local neighborhoods, markets, parks, cultural experiences and rest periods. Avoid treating every free hour as a booking opportunity.

## Live information
Restaurant availability, opening hours, events and temporary closures should be retrieved from live sources when possible.

# Itinerary Optimization

## Good itinerary properties
- Feasible timing.
- Geographic coherence.
- Reasonable activity density.
- Adequate meal/rest periods.
- Buffer for transport and queues.
- Alignment with budget and traveler preferences.
- Clear alternatives when weather or closures disrupt the plan.

## Optimization sequence
1. Lock hard constraints: dates, traveler count, must-do items and budget ceiling.
2. Secure high-risk/time-sensitive items.
3. Group attractions geographically.
4. Minimize unnecessary transfers.
5. Add optional activities after essentials fit.
6. Validate total cost and timing.

## Replanning
When one constraint changes, selectively re-run only affected specialists. Example: if the hotel budget is reduced, re-run hotel search, budget calculation and itinerary generation rather than restarting unrelated weather or flight research.

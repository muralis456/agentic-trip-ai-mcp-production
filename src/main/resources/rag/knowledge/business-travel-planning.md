# Business Travel Planning

## Priorities
- Arrival reliability.
- Proximity to meeting locations.
- Flexible fares and hotel cancellation.
- Fast airport transfers.
- Reliable internet/workspace.
- Time-zone management.
- Rest after long-haul travel.

## Itinerary principle
Optimize for meeting readiness rather than maximum sightseeing. Avoid scheduling critical meetings immediately after high-risk connections when alternatives exist.
